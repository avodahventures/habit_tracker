Faith Habit Tracker — Support

Faith Habit Tracker is a Christian habit-tracking app by Avodah Ventures, pairing daily/weekly habits with Scripture memorization, a prayer journal, and guided devotional plans.

Email us at developer@avodah.ventures and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

How does the free trial work?
Every user gets 30 days of full Premium access automatically, starting from your first time opening the app — no payment required to start. After 30 days, you can continue Premium for $4.99/year or keep using the free version.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple ID. Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Faith Habit Tracker to manage or cancel anytime.

Is my data backed up anywhere?
Your habits, journal entries, and memorized verses are stored privately on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
