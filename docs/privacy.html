Privacy Policy

Faith Habit Tracker — by Avodah Ventures LLC — Last updated: November 2026

Faith Habit Tracker ("the app") is built to keep your habit, journal, and Scripture memorization data private and on your own device. This policy explains what little data is involved and how it's used.

Data stored on your device

Your habits, completion history, prayer journal entries, memorized verses, reminder settings, theme preference, and the optional name you enter for a personal greeting are all stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. None of this is transmitted to Avodah Ventures or stored on any server we control.

Bible verse lookups

When you look up a verse or passage that isn't already in the app's local verse bank, the app may request the public-domain King James Version text from bible-api.com, a third-party service. Only the verse reference you requested (e.g. "John 3:16") is sent — no personal information.

Subscriptions and payments

Faith Habit Tracker offers an optional Premium subscription. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store your payment card details. To confirm and manage your subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service, which receives your App Store transaction/receipt data for this purpose. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for details on their handling of this data.

Notifications

Habit reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not operate a push notification server and do not collect any data through notifications.

What we don't do

  • We don't require account creation or login.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised date above.
